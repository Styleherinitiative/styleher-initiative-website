The outreach

Supporting students beyond the classroom

StyleHer Initiative's Tutorial Centres Outreach was presented as an opportunity to support young people through the gifting of outfits, teaching and empowerment, alongside conversations that encourage confidence and personal growth.

Our campaign materials highlighted donations of clothes and shoes, volunteer participation, and support from community partners. The outreach reflects our belief that useful clothing, practical knowledge and encouragement can work together to make a difference.

Focus areas

Clothing support: Connecting useful clothing and shoes with people who need them.

Teaching and empowerment: Sharing knowledge and practical encouragement.

Community participation: Bringing together donors, volunteers and supporters.
