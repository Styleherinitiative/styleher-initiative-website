Clothing, confidence and community in Eleweeran.

A gathering focused on giving clothes new homes, sharing practical fashion skills and creating space for connection.

July 2026Eleweeran Community, Ile-IfeClothing & skills outreach
The story

More than a clothing giveaway

In July 2026, StyleHer Initiative held its second outreach in Eleweeran Community, Ile-Ife. The gathering brought people together around a simple idea: clothing can be useful for longer, and sharing what we have can make a meaningful difference.

Participants received clothing, explored ways to dress well and make thoughtful use of what they own, and learned practical skills. Volunteers helped bring the activities and distribution together.

What happened

Clothing redistribution: Quality clothing found new homes.

Practical learning: Participants learned about dressing well and useful fashion skills.

Community connection: Volunteers and participants shared an experience centred on care and encouragement.

The people behind the outreach

From those who donated clothing to the volunteers who helped organise and distribute it, this outreach was made possible through collective effort.
